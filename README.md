# HR-Analytics-Attrition-Dashboard
HR Analytics project to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Dashboard** project focuses on analyzing employee attrition to understand why employees leave an organization and to identify workforce trends. The goal is to help HR teams make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall employee attrition.
* Identify factors contributing to employee turnover.
* Examine attrition across departments and job roles.
* Explore the impact of age, salary, job satisfaction, and years of experience on attrition.
* Provide insights to support employee retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data analysis and dashboard creation.
* **Pivot Tables & Charts** – Data summarization and visualization.
* **Data Cleaning** – Preparing employee data for analysis.
* **Interactive Dashboard** – Presenting HR metrics and insights.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Employees
* Total Employees Who Left
* Employee Attrition Rate
* Average Employee Age
* Department-wise Attrition
* Job Role-wise Attrition

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

* **Department Analysis:** Compare attrition across departments.
* **Job Role Analysis:** Identify roles with higher employee turnover.
* **Age Analysis:** Explore attrition patterns across age groups.
* **Salary Analysis:** Examine the relationship between salary and attrition.
* **Job Satisfaction:** Analyze attrition in relation to employee satisfaction.
* **Experience Analysis:** Explore attrition based on years at the company.

## 💡 Business Benefits

* Helps HR teams identify employee turnover patterns.
* Supports data-driven employee retention strategies.
* Highlights workforce trends for better planning.
* Provides an easy-to-understand visual summary of HR metrics.
